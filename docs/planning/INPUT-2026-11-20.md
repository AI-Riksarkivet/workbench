## (a) What the product is for

- **The product.** "One repository and one product, like FiftyOne: explore and search datasets (the viewer, search and
  the explorer UI); annotate them (the annotator and its assist runner); run model pipelines over them (flows and its
  studio UI)" (issue #1, filed on the owner's word "One workbench repo", 2026-11-06).
- **Where it runs.** "as a client for rask, or without rask with lance-namespace or a Lance table directly" (owner,
  2026-11-16; ADR 0001).
- **The job a person does**, in the owner's words from rask's design notes (`OPEN-WORK.md`, "Design — annotation
  projects", quoted in the 2026-11-18 backlog audit): "Annotate should be more like annotate-project and not the
  gallery. We don't pick individual like that. More that we select from search or elsewhere and send to annotate."
  So the job runs: explore or search a dataset, select, send to a project, claim, label, review, publish, and a consumer
  (training) reads the published table.
- **After publish** (owner, 2026-11-18): "Export or a table in rask or s3" (ADR 0013).
- **Who the users are, and which real dataset comes first, are not recorded anywhere** (questions 1 and 2 below).
